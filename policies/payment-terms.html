How Payments Work

Spark Scout connects Brands, Agencies, and Creators and offers tools to help manage collaborations and payments. However, Spark Scout does not process payments directly. All payments are handled by third-party payment providers.

  • Spark Scout does not hold, store, or act as a bank for your funds.
  • Payments depend on external processors, so timing, availability, and success can vary.
  • You are responsible for complying with the third-party provider's rules.

No Guarantee of Payment

  • A buyer, brand, or agency will make or complete any payment.
  • A creator will receive payment for work performed.
  • Funds will be released by a specific date or at all.

Payments rely on users fulfilling their agreements, completing deliverables, and the processes of third-party payment providers.

Escrow-Like Features and Payment Workflows

Some Spark Scout tools may resemble escrow, milestones, or hold/release mechanisms to help manage project payments. However:

  • Spark Scout is not an escrow agent or financial intermediary.
  • We do not guarantee that funds will be held securely or released as expected.
  • We do not enforce private agreements between users or decide who is entitled to funds.

Holds, Delays, Reviews, and Restrictions

Payments, funds, or accounts may be delayed, held, reviewed, or restricted (sometimes for extended periods) due to:

  • Suspected fraud, unusual activity, or security concerns.
  • Disputes or claims between users.
  • Identity or compliance verification requirements.
  • Legal, regulatory, or platform policy obligations.

Chargebacks, Disputes, and Reversals

If a payment is charged back, disputed, or reversed:

  • The recipient may need to return the funds.
  • Spark Scout may deduct amounts from future payouts or balances.
  • Additional fees (such as chargeback processing fees) may apply.

Account Balance

Disputes Between Users

Spark Scout does not mediate or resolve disputes between users. We may hold or release funds for compliance or platform integrity, but we do not determine legal rights or obligations.
