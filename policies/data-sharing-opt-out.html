Data Sharing Opt-Out

Spark Scout may use cookies, analytics tools, pixels, and similar technologies to understand how users interact with the service, improve performance, and support marketing efforts. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to opt out of certain data-sharing activities, including those that may be considered targeted advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising.

Your Opt-Out Choices

You may opt out of certain data-sharing activities by using available privacy controls, cookie preferences, device settings, or by contacting Spark Scout directly through the designated support or contact channels.

How to Submit a Request

  1. Use any available cookie or privacy preference tools provided on Spark Scout websites or applications.
  2. Adjust device or browser privacy settings to limit ad tracking or block certain cookies.
  3. Submit a request through Spark Scout Support (support@sparkscout.com) and include enough information for us to understand and process your request.

What Happens After You Opt Out

After we receive and process an applicable opt-out request, we will make commercially reasonable efforts to honor your request in accordance with applicable law. Some functionality may still rely on essential cookies or operational data needed to provide the service.

Verification

We may need to verify your identity or request details before completing certain privacy requests. This helps us protect user information and prevent unauthorized actions.

Authorized Agents

If permitted by law, you may be able to designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. Additional verification may be required before such a request can be fulfilled.
